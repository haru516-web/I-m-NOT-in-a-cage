# I'm (NOT) in a Cage. — fog forest prototype

## Reference analysis

- [Bilawal Sidhu: spatial memory across two timelines](https://x.com/bilawalsidhu/status/2103148789728989249) places winter footage inside a 3D scan of the same place in summer. The useful design idea is the hard transition between a walkable, sharply bounded memory-space and the surrounding world. The prototype turns that into a freestanding frame containing a small 3D winter forest; the player's path and nearby ground stay modeled in 3D, while the supplied project image supports only the distant field.
- [Four-dimensional block-universe post](https://x.com/drkhaled6455732/status/2103063907648602507) describes past, present and future as simultaneous slices, while a human experiences them sequentially. This is treated as a visual metaphor, not as an established physics claim. The prototype builds four visible layers containing successive states of the same small tree and path.
- [MIZNOM: spatially editable video](https://x.com/MIZNOM/status/2107048154793144578) describes combining two cameras, 3D and AI to preserve the spatial structure of captured footage. The prototype translates that into a road whose 3D route remains legible as it breaks into floating scan-like cubes. It is a hand-built Blender interpretation, not a reconstruction of the post's capture pipeline.

## What is modeled

- A first-person walking path with 3D ground, stones and nearby grass.
- A real depressed well with an inner rock wall and a dark bottom, partly concealed by vegetation.
- An oversized tree/root structure descending from above, plus forest silhouettes and tall reeds.
- A walk-in memory frame containing a separate, snow-covered 3D scene.
- The four-layer time block and the spatial-scan road made from modeled geometry.
- A small sheep facing back toward the player.
- The packed source image `仮イメージ１.jpg`, used as a distant background card in the first-person render. It is hidden from the overview render.

The scene is organized into named Blender collections and includes first-person and overview cameras. No generated image was used.

## Scope

This is a visual blockout for discussing composition and how the references could become places in the game. The geometry is intentionally simple and stylized. The Blender file does not implement step counting, player controls, temporal world simulation, or a production photogrammetry/AI reconstruction pipeline.
